Who We Are

Organization, vision, and mission

Kesate Berhan – Integrated Developmental Organization (KBIDO) is a board-led, non-profit association established in 2025 and headquartered in Mekelle, Tigray. Founded under the auspices of the Tigray Orthodox Church St. Frumentius Theological College, we unite spiritual principles with practical community action to foster human flourishing in post-conflict Tigray.

Vision

A peaceful and just Tigray where individuals and communities live with dignity, resilience, and collective human flourishing.

Mission

To promote sustainable peace, ethical leadership, and human development — providing psychological, ethical, and civic education; empowering vulnerable populations toward self-reliance and dignity; and mobilizing resources and partnerships for holistic community recovery and inclusive development.

What We Do

Five intervention areas, one purpose

KBIDO's programs are carried out through volunteers and church partnerships spanning every zone of Tigray.

Psychosocial Support

Care for individuals and families carrying the psychological weight of conflict and displacement.

Peacebuilding

Community-level reconciliation work rooted in shared dignity and long-term social recovery.

Ethical & Civic Education

Programs in ethical leadership and civic responsibility for the next generation of community leaders.

Disability Inclusion

Working to ensure people with disabilities are fully included in community life and recovery.

Sustainable Livelihoods

Helping vulnerable populations build durable, self-reliant income and livelihood pathways.

Carried out with volunteers whose expertise spans psychology, theology, education, social work, and community development.

Staff Capacity & Reach

Small core team, wide community network

KBIDO operates with a lean core team supported by a large volunteer base with deep humanitarian, peacebuilding, and community development experience — including with national and international NGOs.
